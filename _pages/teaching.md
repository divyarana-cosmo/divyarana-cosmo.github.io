## Teaching

- **Co-lecturer, _Weak Gravitational Lensing_**, Leiden University (2026).
- **Invited lecturer, one-week weak-lensing course with tutorials**, [Astronomy Twinning Program](https://sites.google.com/site/astrotwincolo/meetings/2024-12th-meeting) between Leiden Observatory and Universidad de Antioquia, Medellín, Colombia (2024; funded by Erasmus+). The lectures covered galaxy shape measurement and its use in astrophysics and cosmology, and in the tutorials students built their own pipelines for measuring and modelling weak-lensing signals.
