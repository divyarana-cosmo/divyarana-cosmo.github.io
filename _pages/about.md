I am an observational cosmologist at Leiden Observatory, working with Henk Hoekstra. I use weak gravitational lensing and galaxy clustering to connect galaxies to the dark matter halos they live in, across a wide range of scales: from the stellar mass budget inside galaxies, through the outer edges of halos, to cosmological parameters from cosmic shear and gravitational-wave sirens.

I coordinate the galaxy–galaxy lensing Key Project for **Euclid Data Release 1**, and I am a member of the Euclid Consortium, the Hyper Suprime-Cam (HSC) collaboration and the LIGO Scientific Collaboration. Recent highlights include a forecast showing that small-scale lensing with Euclid can measure the stellar initial mass function to about 8.5% precision, an independent photometric-redshift calibration for HSC Year 3 cosmic shear, and measurements of the splashback radius that marks the edge of galaxy-cluster halos. You can find more on my [research]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}) page.

I received my PhD in Physics from IUCAA, Pune, in 2023, supervised by Surhud More, after an integrated MSc at NISER. At Leiden I co-supervise a PhD student and mentor summer and undergraduate students; two of my former summer students are now PhD students at Stanford and Durham. More on [teaching & mentoring]({{ '/teaching/' | relative_url }}).
